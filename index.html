Capabilities

A complete portfolio of engineered pressure control products and 24-hour field services — from spud to production.

01

Frac Stacks

10K and 15K pressure ratings, gate valves in serviceless, traditional, hydraulic, and manual configurations for high-volume frac operations.

02

Zipper Manifolds

10K and 15K pressure ratings with the same valve options as our frac stacks — engineered for multi-well pad operations.

03

FastLine Manifolds

Single full-bore flow path enabling continuous pumping. Available in 7-1/16" with 15K pressure rating for high-volume pads.

04

QuickLock BOP Adapter

Reduce cellar exposure and save 2–3 hours of rig time per connection. Interfaces seamlessly with our offline cementing system.

05

Wellheads & Tubing Heads

Conventional wellheads, multi-bowl systems, QuickSet wellheads, and a complete line of tubing heads, hangers, seals, and bushings.

06

High-Pressure Flex Hoses

2" and 2.5" inner diameter, 15K pressure rating. Bonded 3" couplings. Hammer union 1502, flange, and Titan clamp end connections.

07

FastLine Remote Lines

7-1/16" API remote lines designed to integrate with our frac stacks, zipper manifolds, and FastLine manifolds.

08

Automation

Automation that works seamlessly across our frac stacks, zipper manifolds, and FastLine manifolds. Reduce operator exposure, increase consistency.

What We Do

Equipment is half the job. Crews are the other half.

01

Frac Watch

On-location personnel monitoring frac operations from rig-up through final flush. Eyes on every connection, every pressure cycle, every transition.

  • Pre-job equipment inspection & rig-up support
  • Continuous on-location monitoring
  • Trained personnel familiar with all Titan equipment
  • Coordination with frac crew & company man
02

Torque & Test

Field torque and pressure testing on wellheads, frac stacks, and pressure-control equipment — in-shop or on customer location.

  • Field-calibrated torque verification
  • Hydrostatic and function testing
  • Documentation and test certificates
  • Available on-call across all three regions
03

Greasing

Programs designed to keep valves cycling smoothly under pressure. Routine and on-call service for production trees, frac stacks, and zipper manifolds.

  • Prevents valve lock-up and galling under pressure
  • Ensures smooth operation on every job
  • Protects critical sealing surfaces
  • Extends equipment life, reduces unplanned downtime
04

Valve Repair & Testing

Complete valve service and repair from qualified technicians. Most valves in the market today can be serviced — in our shop or on your location.

  • Disassembly, inspection, and rebuild
  • API 6A material verification
  • In-shop or on-location service
  • Full test documentation included
