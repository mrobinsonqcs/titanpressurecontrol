Regions Served

Service crews built around the operators we work for.

Field service crews deploy from Midland to wherever our customers are working — spanning three of the most active oil and gas basins in North America.

Permian Basin

The largest active oil-producing basin in the United States — and Titan's home turf. Our headquarters in Midland sits at the heart of the basin, with rapid response times across both the Midland and Delaware sub-basins.

Bakken

Field service crews supporting operators in the Bakken — North Dakota and Eastern Montana. Equipment shipped from Midland and crews deployed to support frac and production work in the basin.

COVERAGE North Dakota · Eastern Montana

Rockies

Service to operators across the Rockies, including the DJ Basin and surrounding plays in Colorado and Wyoming. Crews deployed for both pressure-control equipment installation and ongoing field service.
