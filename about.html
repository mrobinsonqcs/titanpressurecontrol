Who We Are

Engineered for the basin. Backed by the people who run it.

Titan Pressure Control is an oilfield equipment and services company headquartered in Midland, Texas — serving the Permian, Bakken, and Rockies with engineered pressure control solutions and 24-hour field services.

Our Approach

Pressure control built for the field.

Titan exists because pressure control is too important to be treated as a commodity. The equipment has to be right. The people on location have to know what they're doing. The service has to be there at 2 AM on a Sunday, not just during business hours.

We build, sell, and service a complete portfolio of pressure-control equipment — from frac stacks and zipper manifolds to wellheads, production trees, and the components that connect them all. And we back every piece of equipment with field crews who know it cold.

From spud to production, our customers know what they're getting: equipment that performs and people who answer the phone.

What Sets Us Apart

We don't just sell pressure control. We run it.

Most pressure-control suppliers stop at the equipment. Titan doesn't. Our field service teams — Frac Watch, Torque & Test, Greasing, Valve Repair — work alongside our customers' crews on location, ensuring every piece of Titan equipment performs the way it's supposed to.

That partnership matters. When operators decide who they want on the next pad, they don't just pick equipment — they pick the company that's going to be there when something needs attention. Titan is built around being that company.
