Field Services

Crews who know the equipment cold.

Equipment is half the job. The other half is the crew that installs it, the techs who service it, and the people on call when something needs attention at 2 AM. Titan's service teams operate across the Permian, Bakken, and Rockies — available 24/7.

01

Frac Watch

On-location personnel monitoring frac operations from rig-up through final flush. Trained to know every Titan product on location, coordinated with your frac crew and company man, present for every connection and pressure cycle.

  • Pre-job equipment inspection & rig-up support
  • Continuous on-location monitoring through frac
  • Familiar with all Titan equipment on location
  • Coordination with frac crew & company man
  • Available 24/7 across all three regions
02

Torque & Test

Field torque verification and pressure testing on wellheads, frac trees, and pressure-control equipment. Performed in our shop or on your location. Documentation and test certificates included.

  • Field-calibrated torque verification
  • Hydrostatic and function testing
  • Documentation and test certificates
  • In-shop or on customer location
  • Available on-call across all regions
03

Greasing

Greasing programs designed to keep valves cycling smoothly under pressure. Routine and on-call service for production trees, frac stacks, and zipper manifolds.

  • Prevents valve lock-up & galling under pressure
  • Ensures smooth operation on every job
  • Protects critical sealing surfaces
  • Extends equipment life, reduces unplanned downtime
  • Programs scheduled around your operations
04

Valve Repair & Testing

Complete valve service and repair from qualified technicians. Most valves on the market today can be serviced and rebuilt — in our Midland shop or on your location. Full test documentation included.

  • Disassembly, inspection, and rebuild
  • API 6A material verification
  • In-shop or on-location service
  • Full test documentation included
  • Most valves on the market today supported
